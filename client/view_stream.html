🎥 ESP32-CAM Video Stream Viewer

Real-time video streaming from your ESP32-CAM module

📹

Enter ESP32-CAM IP address and click "Connect" to start

ℹ️ How to Use:

  • Enter your ESP32-CAM IP address (find it in Serial Monitor)
  • Click "Connect" to establish connection
  • Click "Start Stream" to begin video streaming
  • Click "Capture Image" to download a single frame
  • Click "Stop Stream" to pause the video feed
